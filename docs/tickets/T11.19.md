**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude and approved by the owner, 2026-11-26)
- [ ] Above the draft cards of a run with at least two food cards (T11.5's drafts from messages and T11.10's from a photo or a voice note, both through `views/drafts.card_form()`), one "Meal for all food cards" selector sets the meal slot of every food card in one choice; a card's own selector still changes that card alone afterwards - AppTests: one choice fills every food card; a later change on one card leaves the others; Approve saves each card's own slot.
- [ ] A slot the model read from the text (e.g. "Breakfast: ...") is kept until the gymer picks one for all; with one food card, or none, the selector is not shown; weight and workout cards do not change - AppTests.
- [ ] When Save decisions is refused because an approved card is incomplete or out of range (e.g. a food card with no grams), the error names that card by its number and what it shows (e.g. "Card 2 (Boiled eggs): enter an amount from 0.1 to 2000 g") and is shown at that card as well as above the cards; nothing is saved, as now - AppTests for a food card with no grams and a weight out of range, in a run of several cards (added by the owner on 2026-11-26, after T11.10's live check).
- [ ] Nothing else changes: the cards, what Approve saves, the store and what reaches a model stay as they are (no store change, no new model call) - the existing tests of T11.5 and T11.10 pass unchanged.
- [ ] Texts in English and German.
- [ ] A progress entry; no compliance run is needed, as no data collected, stored or sent changes (its PR description says so).
**Notes:** P2, cut early. Seen in T11.10's live check (2026-11-26): a photo of one meal gave several food cards, each asking for its meal slot, because a photo names no meal. The card keys are per card (`views/drafts.py`) and the cards sit in an `st.form`, where Streamlit allows no `on_change` callback but the submit button's; so the shared selector sits above the form and writes each card's meal key in its callback, before the cards are drawn (a widget's state cannot change after the widget is drawn in the same run). Left for the Question phase (code review round 1 of NMH1988/fitkompass#52): whether a rerun from the selector keeps a card's edits not yet submitted (if not, the selector is used before any card is edited, or the meal selectors leave the form); and that a card whose key is set from the callback no longer passes `index=`, or Streamlit warns of a default value set through the Session State API too. Guessing the slot from the time of day was considered and not proposed: a wrong guess saved in one click is worse than an empty selector. In the same live check a voice note's card for "two boiled eggs" had no grams (a count, not an amount) and was approved with the other cards; the save was refused with the food log's range message above the form, naming no card, so the owner had to search for it. The refusal itself is right (the workflow checks every approved card again); only where and how it is said changes.
