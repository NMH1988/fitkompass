### T11.14 (P1) The client's messages in the weekly review draft (M) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#29
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 2 steps 1-3 (the weekly review workflow gains a database query and multi-step reasoning over the thread), E2b, E3; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.6, O25
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude at the owner's request and approved by the owner on 2026-11-23, with the choices in the Notes)
- [ ] **The review reads the client's messages of its week.** When a coach starts a weekly review (T9.16, D17) and the client's consent (b) for this coach is given, the draft step also gets the client's own messages of the review's week (Monday to Sunday in `Europe/Berlin`, at most `config.THREAD_MESSAGES_TO_MODEL`, never the coach's messages), each through the input guard and the domain guardrails first, a rejected one left out and counted; without consent (b) the review runs as today, with no message, and the coach is told the messages were not used - graph tests with a scripted fake model (with and without consent, a rejected message never reaching the model).
- [ ] **No message text in the review's checkpoints.** The messages are read from the store inside the draft step and passed to the model there; `ReviewState`, checkpointed in the SqliteSaver file (D12), keeps only the IDs of the messages used and the count left out, never their text, on a first draft and on a second one after failed checks - a test that the checkpointer file holds none of the canary messages after a run.
- [ ] **What the review says from a message is marked and checked.** A statement taken from the client's messages is a statement of its own kind ("the client reported", e.g. poor sleep or a busy week), which cites the IDs of the messages it comes from; code refuses a draft whose statement of that kind cites a message that was not passed to it, or that states a number the cited messages do not hold; a recommendation still needs the metrics or a cited source as today, never a message alone; the coach and, once approved, the gymer see such a statement labelled "from the client's messages" in English and German - `src/weekly_review.py` validation tests, AppTest.
- [ ] **Consent (b)'s text** adds this use (the client's own messages of the week in their coach's weekly review drafts, kept in the approved review the client reads), so its version changes and the gymer is asked again before the next flow (T11.5's rule) - AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] The approved review keeps such statements as it keeps the others (the reviews table, in both exports, deleted with the review's data), and `docs/PRD.md`'s "Newly stored" names them; the field descriptions the model reads for the new kind are approved by the owner (O25) - store tests, review.
- [ ] No log line or trace holds a message or a statement drawn from one (canary tests in `tests/test_observability.py`, a case in `tests/test_tracing.py`); every Sprint 3 guardrail of the review still holds (T9.16's and T10.2's checks).
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-55 holds; `code-reviewer`, `/security-review`, `compliance-reviewer`, and their reports on the PR.
