**Notes:** In T11.10's live check (2026-11-26) a synthetic voice note about a knee injury ended `nothing`, with no guard line in the log. The photo instruction's NONE ("no clearly identifiable food or beverages") is left as it is: a photo with no food has nothing for the guard to read. The instruction is the owner's text (O19, `.claude/rules/workflow.md`: the owner writes prompts first), hence O27.
