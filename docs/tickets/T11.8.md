### T11.8 The coach's recipe library and the clients' ratings (M) · status: todo · issue not yet created
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 2 step 2 (the data the dish workflow uses), E1a; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.7 (order only)
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude, 2026-11-19; for the owner's approval)
- [ ] A coach saves recipes (a name, USDA foods with grams, steps in their own words, with length limits from config); their current clients read them; the steps are rendered as Markdown without HTML; the page says the app does not check allergens or the coach's qualifications, and credits the USDA data next to the values - store tests, AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] The demo mode's seed recipes (names, ingredients, steps) are written by us or the owner, not copied or closely paraphrased from a recipe source, with no image - review.
- [ ] A client rates a recipe 1 to 5 stars only if they are a client of its coach and logged its dish; one rating per client and recipe (a new rating replaces their earlier one by a new row, as the store keeps rows, and the newest counts); every other case gets the same `NotFound` - store tests in the refused-cases matrix.
- [ ] The coach sees a recipe's average and count only once at least the minimum number of clients in config have rated it (below it, "not enough ratings yet"), and is never shown who rated; the rating screen says, in English and German, that with few clients the coach may still work out a rating, and `docs/SECURITY.md` records that limit (owner's choice, 2026-11-19) - store tests at the minimum and one below it, AppTest.
- [ ] Recipes are in the coach's export, ratings in the gymer's; both are deleted with their owner's data, and a recipe's ratings go with it - store tests.
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-48 holds; `code-reviewer`, `compliance-reviewer` (new stored data, the coach's own content shown to others), and their reports on the PR.
**Notes:** no recipe dataset is imported (`.claude/rules/copyright.md`, rule 2; `docs/PRD.md`, feature 33). If time runs short the ratings are cut and the recipes stay (T11.9 needs them).
