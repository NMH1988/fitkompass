### T11.8 The coach's recipe library and the clients' ratings (M) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#15
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 2 step 2 (the data the dish workflow uses), E1a; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.7 (order only)
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude and approved by the owner, 2026-11-19, with the choices in the Notes)
- [ ] A coach saves recipes (a name, USDA foods with grams, steps in their own words, with length limits from config); their current clients read them; the steps are rendered as Markdown without HTML; the page says the app does not check allergens or the coach's qualifications, and credits the USDA data next to the values - store tests, AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] The demo mode's seed recipes (names, ingredients, steps) are written by us or the owner, not copied or closely paraphrased from a recipe source, with no image - review.
- [ ] A gymer logs a recipe of their current coach from the recipe page (in a click, at the recipe's grams or scaled by code to a portion they choose); each meal item is marked as logged from that recipe (`from_recipe`, a migration, holding the recipe's ID with no foreign key, and `recipes` keyed with `AUTOINCREMENT` so a deleted recipe's ID is never reused by another), set only after the store has checked that the recipe belongs to the gymer's current coach (as T9.34's `from_suggestion`), kept by an edit only while the food stays the same, in the gymer's export and deleted with their data; when the coach deletes their data, the gymer's logged entries stay as they are, and a mark whose recipe is gone allows no rating; T11.9 reuses this path to log a dish - store tests (the coach's delete succeeds and leaves the gymer's log and marks intact; a new recipe never gets a deleted one's ID), AppTest. This logging path was proposed by Claude in the fixes of code review round 7 of T11.1 and confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-19.
- [ ] The cross-user reads, recorded in `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` with the owner's choices: the gymer reads their current coach's recipes by the relationship (like `users.display_name_of()`); the coach's read of a recipe's average and count, built from gymers' rows, counts a rating only while its client shares nutrition with the coach (so the count cannot tell the coach that a non-sharing client logged the dish) and is not audited, as it holds no single person's value and shows only from the minimum (a recorded exception to "every coach read is audited"); every new store function is classified in `tests/test_store_access.py` - store tests in the refused-cases matrix or a group of their own (a rating of a client who stopped sharing nutrition not counted), review.
- [ ] A client rates a recipe 1 to 5 stars only if they are a client of its coach and logged its dish (a meal item marked `from_recipe` for it); one rating per client and recipe (a new rating replaces their earlier one by a new row, as the store keeps rows, and the newest counts); every other case gets the same `NotFound` - store tests in the refused-cases matrix.
- [ ] The coach sees a recipe's average and count only once at least the minimum number of clients in config (3) have rated it (below it, "not enough ratings yet"), and is never shown who rated; the rating screen says, in English and German, that with few clients the coach may still work out a rating, and `docs/SECURITY.md` records that limit (owner's choice, 2026-11-19) - store tests at the minimum and one below it, AppTest.
- [ ] Recipes are in the coach's export, ratings in the gymer's; both are deleted with their owner's data, and a recipe's ratings go with it; `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` ("Personal data", "The store") names recipes, ratings and the `from_recipe` mark among the stored data - store tests, review.
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-48 holds; `code-reviewer`, `compliance-reviewer` (new stored data, the coach's own content shown to others), and their reports on the PR.
**Notes:** no recipe dataset is imported (`.claude/rules/copyright.md`, rule 2; `docs/PRD.md`, feature 33). If time runs short the ratings are cut and the recipes stay (T11.9 needs them). **The owner's choices (2026-11-19):** logging a recipe from the recipe page confirmed; a rating counts only while its client shares nutrition; the coach's read of the average and count is not audited (recorded exception); the minimum is 3 raters.
