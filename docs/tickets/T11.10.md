### T11.10 (P1) A photo or a voice note to a draft log entry (L) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#17
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 4 step 1 (image and text analysis), Case 4 step 4 in part; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.5, O19
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude and approved by the owner, 2026-11-19, with the choices in the Notes)
- [ ] The model (D29) is chosen in the Research phase from the provider family the app already uses (OpenAI models through OpenRouter), so consent (c) names no new provider, its image (and, for voice, audio) input and its price confirmed, added to the model allowlist and built only with `build_chat_model()`; its licence and terms (input data, any condition on its output, its training data where `.claude/rules/copyright.md` rule 9 asks) are recorded in `docs/SOURCES.md` or `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` with the owner's approval - review.
- [ ] Only with the gymer's consent (c) (`docs/PRD.md`, the Capstone's consents; its text names photos and voice notes, OpenRouter and this model's provider; stored with the version of its text and checked again right before each model call through T11.5's consent functions), a photo (uploaded as JPG or PNG with `st.file_uploader`, or taken with `st.camera_input`) or a voice note within the limits in config (a photo at most 5 MB, downscaled when re-encoded; a voice note at most 60 seconds) becomes a draft card through T11.5's checks; a file over the limit or of another type is refused before any model call - tests with a fake model.
- [ ] The steps run in this order: the size and type check; a first model call that only transcribes the voice note's spoken words or describes the foods, drinks and amounts in the photo (a fixed instruction, drafted by the owner under O19, that asks for nothing else), with no profile, message or other data; then the input guard and the domain guardrails on that text, before T11.5's extraction; a rejected transcript or description makes no draft and no further model call - tests with a scripted fake model (an injury and an injection in the transcript among them). The first call is the one model call that no guard can check first, as the guard reads text: it is recorded as an exception in `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` ("Guards") with the owner's decision, and as a known limit in `docs/SECURITY.md` (the whole file reaches OpenRouter and the provider before any check, people, documents or labels in it included). Consent (c)'s text and the upload's help say so, in English and German, and ask the gymer to keep people, documents and labels out of the photo and to record only their own voice - AppTest.
- [ ] The file, its transcript and its description are kept in memory only: none is written to disk, a log line or a trace, also while demo mode's unmasked traces are on (`tracing.demo_gate_open()`) - tests (a temp directory left empty, canary tests on the file and the transcript, a trace case with the gate open).
- [ ] A photo is re-encoded in memory (Pillow) without its metadata before it is sent; a voice note is taken only from the browser's recording widget (`st.audio_input`), never an uploaded audio file, and re-encoded in memory without tags - tests that a JPEG with a GPS tag leaves the app without it and that the audio sent carries no tags.
- [ ] Nothing is logged without the gymer's approval; the card is labelled as AI-generated - AppTest.
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-51 holds; `code-reviewer`, `/security-review` (a new input path to a model), `compliance-reviewer` (a new model provider and special category data in images and audio), and their reports on the PR.
