### T11.16 (P2) Client notes: the coach's AI remembers what a client said, with the coach's approval and the client's consent (L) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#31
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 2 step 3 (long-term memory), steps 1-2, E2b, E3; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.6, O25
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude at the owner's request, "layer 3", and approved by the owner on 2026-11-23, then changed after code review round 1 and compliance run 1 of NMH1988/fitkompass#33 with the owner's choices of the same day, in the Notes; it changes an owner's decision, below)
- [ ] **A consent of its own, which alone opens this flow.** Notes are drafted, kept and used only with the gymer's consent (e), a new purpose in T11.5's consents table, given for one coach and kept with that relationship as (b) is (if T11.5's table keeps the relationship only for (b), this ticket extends it with a migration of its own, every row kept); consent (b) neither opens nor is needed for this flow. Its text names the coach, what is kept, each tool that uses the notes (the coach's AI chat, the weekly review draft, the suggested foods draft), where it is sent (OpenRouter and the model provider), that a food preference note can reveal health or beliefs (a vegetarian or halal diet, say), that refusing changes nothing else in the coaching, and that a weekly review statement or a suggested foods list built while the notes existed stays after they are deleted (as T11.14's statements stay after (b)) - store tests per case (no (e), (e) of an ended relationship, withdrawn), AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] **Deleted when the consent or the relationship ends.** Withdrawing (e), or the end of the relationship (read as a withdrawal of (e)), deletes that coach's notes about the gymer in the same transaction, then checkpoints the store's write-ahead log (`db.checkpoint()`), as `my_data.delete_user_data()` and `records.delete_record()` do; a former coach never reads a note - store tests (each ending leaves no note row; the `-wal` file holds no canary note after the checkpoint).
- [ ] **Drafted from the thread, approved by the coach.** On a client's page the coach asks for note drafts; the graph reads the client's own messages of the thread (never the coach's, as T11.14 and T11.15 do) through T11.6's guarded read (with (e) in place of (b)'s use, a rejected message left out and the count shown in code) and proposes notes with structured output, each of a kind from a fixed list in config (food preference, food dislike, eating schedule, training schedule and equipment, goal context), at most `config.MAX_NOTE_CHARS` characters, citing the IDs of the messages it comes from (code refuses a citation of a message not passed). A note is dropped when it is of no listed kind, when the domain guardrails reject it (`src/guardrails.py`: injury, medical, and a new concern for allergy and intolerance added by this ticket, a guardrail change that needs the compliance run), or when it holds the display name of the coach or of any of the coach's current or former clients (the check `remember_preference` makes; another person's name is a known limit, as in the Capstone's guardrails). The coach approves, edits or rejects each card, and nothing is stored before that - graph tests with scripted proposals (one per drop rule, e.g. "dairy makes me bloated, I think I am lactose intolerant"), AppTest.
- [ ] **Stored as the client's data, written and read by the coach under the store's rules.** The notes table comes with a new migration (`STRICT`, foreign keys on, never cascading). A coach's save is a write into a client's records checked inside its write transaction (the relationship active and (e) given, else the same `NotFound` and no row), with an audit row with no values (a new action, `note_set`, added to `access.Action` and to the `audit_log.action` CHECK by rebuilding `audit_log`, as `schema._V6` did), in `COACH_WRITES` in `tests/test_store_access.py`. The coach reads notes on screen only through one store read with the same check, audited as every coach read is (`read`), in the refused-cases matrix (`REFUSED`, a former coach and a coach without (e) among them); the AI's read of the notes goes through the same read and is audited too, as `get_client_week`'s reads are today (the owner's choice of no audit covers the AI's read of a thread, not of notes). A changed note is a new row (records are never updated in place); the gymer reads every note about them on My data and deletes any of them (`db.checkpoint()` after the delete); the notes are in the gymer's export, are deleted with either side's data (`my_data.delete_user_data()`), and are never put in the vector store - store tests, AppTest.
- [ ] **Used only by that coach's AI, while it is allowed.** The coach's chat gets the notes through a tool or with `get_client_week` (decided in the Question phase), and T11.14 and T11.15 may pass them to their drafts, each only after checking again the relationship and (e) right before the model call; a workflow's checkpointed state (the weekly review's SqliteSaver file, D12) keeps only the IDs of the notes used, never their text, so deleting the notes leaves none in a checkpoint; a new coach never sees an earlier coach's notes - graph tests with a fake model that never receives a note without (e), and a canary test on the review's checkpointer file after a run with notes.
- [ ] **Labels and limits on screen.** Every note shows the coach and the gymer that it was drafted by AI and approved by the coach; the notes' pages say that notes never hold allergies, intolerances, conditions or injuries, so an empty list says nothing about them - AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] **The coach's rules change on the record.** `docs/PRD.md` feature 22 says the coach's long-term memory may now hold notes about a client only through this flow (the coach's own preferences stay as they are, and `remember_preference` still refuses a client's data); `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` names the notes table among the stored data, `note_set` among the audited actions - review.
- [ ] No log line or trace holds a note or a message (canary tests, a case in `tests/test_tracing.py`).
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-57 holds; `code-reviewer`, `/security-review`, `compliance-reviewer`, and their reports on the PR.
