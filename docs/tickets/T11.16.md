### T11.16 (P2) Client notes: the coach's AI remembers what a client said, with the coach's approval and the client's consent (L) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#31
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): Case 2 step 3 (long-term memory), steps 1-2, E2b, E3; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.6, O25
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude at the owner's request, "layer 3", and approved by the owner on 2026-11-23, with the choices in the Notes; it changes an owner's decision, below)
- [ ] **A consent of its own.** Notes are kept only with the gymer's consent (e), a new purpose in T11.5's consents table ("your coach's AI keeps notes from your messages, approved by your coach, and uses them in your coach's AI tools"), given for one coach and ending with that relationship as (b) does; its text names the coach, what is kept and where it is sent (OpenRouter and the model provider); consent (b) does not count for it; withdrawing it deletes that coach's notes about the gymer at once - store tests per case (no (e), (e) of an ended relationship, withdrawn), AppTest in English and German.
- [ ] **Drafted from the thread, approved by the coach.** On a client's page the coach asks for note drafts; the graph reads the thread through T11.6's guarded read (both checks, a rejected message left out and counted) and proposes notes with structured output, each of a kind from a fixed list in config (proposed: food preference, food dislike, eating schedule, training schedule and equipment, goal context), at most `config.MAX_NOTE_CHARS` characters, citing the IDs of the messages it comes from (code refuses a citation of a message not passed); a note of no listed kind, one that the domain guardrails reject (a diagnosis, an injury, a medical condition, an allergy or intolerance) or one that names a person is dropped; the coach approves, edits or rejects each card, and nothing is stored before that - graph tests with scripted proposals, AppTest.
- [ ] **Stored as the client's data, written by the coach.** The notes table comes with a new migration (`STRICT`, foreign keys on, never cascading); a coach's save is a write into a client's records checked inside its write transaction (the relationship active and consent (e) given, else the same `NotFound` and no row), with an audit row with no values (a new action, `note_set`, added to `access.Action` and to the `audit_log.action` CHECK by rebuilding `audit_log`, as `schema._V6` did), and joins `COACH_WRITES` in `tests/test_store_access.py`; a changed note is a new row (records are never updated in place); the gymer reads every note about them on My data and deletes any of them; the notes are in the gymer's export, are deleted with either side's data (`my_data.delete_user_data()`), and are never put in the vector store - store tests, AppTest.
- [ ] **Used only by that coach's AI, while it is allowed.** The coach's chat gets the notes through a tool or with `get_client_week` (decided in the Question phase), and T11.14 and T11.15 may pass them to their drafts, each only after checking again the relationship and consent (e) right before the model call; a new coach never sees an earlier coach's notes - graph tests with a fake model that never receives a note without (e).
- [ ] **The coach's rules change on the record.** `docs/PRD.md` feature 22 says the coach's long-term memory may now hold notes about a client only through this flow (the coach's own preferences stay as they are, and `remember_preference` still refuses a client's data); `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` names the notes table among the stored data and `note_set` among the audited actions - review.
- [ ] No log line or trace holds a note or a message (canary tests, a case in `tests/test_tracing.py`).
- [ ] `docs/PRD.md` AC-57 holds; `code-reviewer`, `/security-review`, `compliance-reviewer`, and their reports on the PR.
