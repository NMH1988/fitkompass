### T11.21 (P2) A food the table lacks: the gymer asks the coach, who answers with a recipe (M) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#50
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): E1a (a food that is not in the USDA table can still be logged); beyond the brief (owner's request, 2026-11-26) for the feature itself; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.8, T11.20 (order only: most missing foods should be found first)
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude and approved by the owner, 2026-11-26; corrected after code review round 1 of NMH1988/fitkompass#52, the same day)
- [ ] A gymer who shares nutrition with a coach they are connected with now asks that coach to add a food the table lacks: from Today's food form, and from a draft card with no match (T11.5, T11.10); on a draft card the field starts with the card's own words for the food only once the gymer presses the button, and the gymer can edit it; the request holds the food's name (plain text, at most a length set in `config`) and an optional note, and nothing else; only what the gymer submits is stored - AppTests from both places; a gymer with no current coach, or who does not share nutrition, sees no button.
- [ ] The request is stored in a new table, written by the gymer, read by the gymer and by the coach through `access.authorised_read()` for the nutrition scope; every coach read is audited, the notifications' count included, unless the owner chooses in the Question phase to extend T9.30's middle way to it (recorded then); every other reader gets the same `NotFound`; queries are constant strings (`tests/test_store_sql_scan.py`); the table is classified in `tests/test_store_access.py` and the refused-cases matrix.
- [ ] The coach's answer is a write into the gymer's record, the fourth such write: it runs `access.authorise_read()` for the nutrition scope inside its write transaction, with the answer and an audit row of a new action (`access.Action` and the `audit_log.action` CHECK, added by a migration that builds `audit_log` again, as `schema._V9` did), and is in `tests/test_store_access.py`'s group of a coach's writes, refused wherever a read is; the coach answers by linking a recipe of their own (an existing one, or a new one made from USDA foods in the recipe library, T11.8) or by closing the request with a short reason from a fixed list - store tests.
- [ ] The coach sees open requests on the client's page and in their notifications (T9.29: the count and a link, never the text in a toast); the gymer sees the answer in their notifications and logs the linked recipe as any recipe of their coach (the store's check that it is the current coach's, the portion factor) - AppTests and store tests.
- [ ] When the relationship ends or the gymer stops sharing nutrition, the coach no longer reads the request (the same `NotFound`) and the gymer sees it as closed; when the coach deletes the linked recipe, the gymer sees the answer without the recipe ("removed"), as a logged recipe's mark stays (T11.8) - store tests and AppTests.
- [ ] Requests and answers are in both sides' exports (`my_data.export_user_data()`, as T9.25's questions and answers are), and deleted with either side's data (`my_data.delete_user_data()`) - tests.
- [ ] No nutrient is typed by hand: a food's values come only from USDA foods, through the coach's recipe; nothing in the request or the answer is sent to a model or the USDA API; the name and the note are shown as plain text (escaped Markdown, no links or images) - tests.
- [ ] Demo mode seeds one open request and one answered with a recipe, with made-up data; texts in English and German.
- [ ] `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` (the fourth write into another user's records, the new audited action), `docs/SECURITY.md` and `docs/PRD.md` record the new stored data and its readers; a compliance run (new stored personal data, including that a draft card's words, from a photo's description or a voice note's transcript that T11.10 keeps off disk, are stored once the gymer submits them), its findings handled as `.claude/rules/workflow.md` says; a progress entry.
**Notes:** The owner's idea in T11.10's live check (2026-11-26): when a food is not in the USDA list, the gymer adds it and the coach checks its nutrition and adds it to the list, so the list grows beyond USDA's. Proposed as above instead of a coach typing nutrients, for three reasons recorded with the owner: (1) copyright: the values a coach would look up elsewhere (other apps' or other countries' food databases) are mostly not openly licensed (`.claude/rules/copyright.md`, rules 1, 2 and 9; Open Food Facts is excluded, ODbL), while a recipe made from USDA foods is the coach's own content (T11.8); (2) quality: values that nobody checked would reach the targets, the calorie floor check and the weekly review; (3) privacy: a shared food list across coaches would mix users' data, so the "list" is each coach's recipe library, read by their own clients only. Left for the Question phase, the owner's choices: whether the coach's notification count is audited (above); whether a draft card's words may prefill the field at all, or the gymer types the name (the compliance run weighs storing words a model read from a photo or a voice note); and a gymer's own foods typed from a package label (values per 100 g, marked as the gymer's own entry, not USDA, seen by the coach under the nutrition scope), which many diet apps offer, with its own guardrail checks and a compliance run. The request table, the coach's answer and the notification follow T9.25's questions and answers (`questions.py`, `review_answered`) and T9.30's notifications.
