[Implementation plan](../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) · Epic 11: Capstone (FitKompass)

### T11.21 (P2) A food the table lacks: the gymer asks the coach, who answers with a recipe (M) · status: todo · issue NMH1988/fitkompass#50
**Serves:** Capstone (`docs/BRIEF_CAPSTONE.md`): E1a (a food that is not in the USDA table can still be logged); beyond the brief (owner's request, 2026-11-26) for the feature itself; Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): none; Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none.
**Depends on:** T11.8, T11.20 (order only: most missing foods should be found first)
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed by Claude and approved by the owner, 2026-11-26)
- [ ] A gymer connected with a coach who shares nutrition with them asks the coach to add a food the table lacks: from Today's food form, and from a draft card with no match (T11.5, T11.10), prefilled with the card's own words for the food, which the gymer can edit; the request holds the food's name (plain text, at most `config` characters) and an optional note, and nothing else - AppTests from both places; a gymer with no current coach, or who does not share nutrition, sees no button.
- [ ] The request is stored in a new table, written only by the gymer, read by the coach through `access.authorised_read()` for the nutrition scope (audited like every coach read) and by the gymer; every other reader gets the same `NotFound`; it is in the gymer's export, deleted with either side's data (`my_data.delete_user_data()`, `export_user_data()`), classified in `tests/test_store_access.py` and the refused-cases matrix; queries are constant strings (`tests/test_store_sql_scan.py`).
- [ ] The coach sees open requests on the client's page and in their notifications (T9.29, the count and a link, never the text in a toast), and answers one by linking a recipe of their own (an existing one, or a new one made from USDA foods in the recipe library, T11.8), or by closing it with a short reason from a fixed list; the gymer sees the answer in their notifications and logs the linked recipe as any recipe of their coach (the store's check that it is the current coach's, the portion factor) - AppTests and store tests.
- [ ] No nutrient is typed by hand: a food's values come only from USDA foods, through the coach's recipe; nothing in the request or the answer is sent to a model or the USDA API; the name and the note are shown as plain text (escaped Markdown, no links or images) - tests.
- [ ] Demo mode seeds one open request and one answered with a recipe, with made-up data; texts in English and German.
- [ ] `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md` and `docs/PRD.md` record the new stored data and its readers; a compliance run (new stored personal data), its findings handled as `.claude/rules/workflow.md` says; a progress entry.
**Notes:** The owner's idea in T11.10's live check (2026-11-26): when a food is not in the USDA list, the gymer adds it and the coach checks its nutrition and adds it to the list, so the list grows beyond USDA's. Proposed as above instead of a coach typing nutrients, for three reasons recorded with the owner: (1) copyright: the values a coach would look up elsewhere (other apps' or other countries' food databases) are mostly not openly licensed (`.claude/rules/copyright.md`, rules 1, 2 and 9; Open Food Facts is excluded, ODbL), while a recipe made from USDA foods is the coach's own content (T11.8); (2) quality: values that nobody checked would reach the targets, the calorie floor check and the weekly review; (3) privacy: a shared food list across coaches would mix users' data, so the "list" is each coach's recipe library, read by their own clients only. Left for the Question phase, the owner's choice: a gymer's own foods typed from a package label (values per 100 g, marked as the gymer's own entry, not USDA, seen by the coach under the nutrition scope), which many diet apps offer; it would need its own guardrail checks and a compliance run. The request table and the notification follow T9.25's questions and T9.30's notifications.
